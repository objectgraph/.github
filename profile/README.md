# ObjectGraph

### Apps for Apple platforms, since day one of the App Store.

ObjectGraph LLC, New York. Founded 2004. **iRetroPhone** shipped on July 10, 2008, the day
the App Store opened, and about 145 apps have followed. Today the work is map and GPS tools,
a seismograph you can hold, and — under the VooV name — an AI workspace.

[**objectgraph.com**](https://www.objectgraph.com) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/developer/objectgraph-llc/id284700705) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Blog](https://www.objectgraph.com/blog) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [VooV / Catalyst →](https://github.com/voovai)

## Products

| Product | What it is | Get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **iRetroPhone** | A rotary dial phone for iPhone and iPad. On the store since opening day 2008; version 3 is a SwiftUI rewrite of the original. | [iretrophone.com](https://iretrophone.com) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id284700702) |
| **Seismograph / iSeismometer** | Your phone as a three-axis seismograph and your Mac as the receiver for a home seismic network. iPhone, iPad and Mac. | [seismograph.com](https://seismograph.com) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id304190739) |
| **Geo Measure** | Area and distance on a map: how big is that farm, how far to the station. | [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id451326903) · [Mac](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id735322124) · [Web](https://www.geosq.com/geomeasure) |
| **Geo Elevation** | The elevation profile of any path or point on a map. | [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id509924746) · [Mac](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1096825035) · [Web](https://www.geosq.com/geoelevation) |
| **Geo Tracker** | Record routes with GPS and keep them as GPX. Everything stays on the device. | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id627038315) |
| **GPX Explore** | A GPX track viewer for iOS and Mac, with elevation-coloured tracks. | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6745435014) |
| **WorkoutGPX** | Turns Apple Health workouts into GPX files. | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6743941036) |
| **Seismica** | A live earthquake map. | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6746057677) |

The AI work — **Catalyst**, every AI in one workspace — lives in the
[voovai](https://github.com/voovai) organization.
